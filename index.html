Project Overview

A Landmark Address on the Southern Bypass

Orizon is a landmark 8.3-acre luxury residential township by Srijan Realty situated directly on Southern EM Bypass. Designed around nature and modern convenience, the project features 6 iconic high-rise towers (G+28 to G+33) offering spacious 3 BHK residences with 80% open green space. Every home features expansive terrace balconies, paired with IGBC Platinum pre-certified eco-friendly architecture and swift connectivity to Shahid Khudiram Metro, Garia, and South Kolkata hubs.
